# 胃肠道症状概述

作者：[Jonathan Gotfried](/professional/authors/gotfried-jonathan), MD, Main Line Health, Bryn Mawr, PA

Reviewed By[Minhhuyen Nguyen](/professional/authors/nguyen-minhhuyen), MD, Fox Chase Cancer Center, Temple University

**上消化道（GI）症状**包括

* [胸痛](/professional/cardiology/symptoms-of-cardiovascular-disorders/chest-pain)
* [慢性反复性腹痛](/professional/gastroenterology/symptoms-of-gastrointestinal-disorders/chronic-abdominal-pain-and-recurrent-abdominal-pain)
* [消化不良](/professional/gastroenterology/symptoms-of-gastrointestinal-disorders/dyspepsia)
* [吞咽困难](/professional/gastroenterology/esophageal-and-swallowing-disorders/dysphagia)
* [癔球症](/professional/gastroenterology/symptoms-of-gastrointestinal-disorders/globus-sensation)
* [口臭](/professional/dentistry/symptoms-of-dental-and-oral-disorders/halitosis)
* [呃逆](/professional/gastroenterology/symptoms-of-gastrointestinal-disorders/hiccups)
* [恶心和呕吐](/professional/gastroenterology/symptoms-of-gastrointestinal-disorders/nausea-and-vomiting)
* [反刍综合征](/professional/gastroenterology/symptoms-of-gastrointestinal-disorders/rumination)

某些上消化道症状属于[肠脑互动障碍](/professional/gastroenterology/approach-to-the-gastrointestinal-patient/disorders-of-gut-brain-interaction)（也称为功能性胃肠病；即经过全面评估后未发现生理性病因）。

**下消化道症状** 包括

* [便秘](/professional/gastroenterology/symptoms-of-gastrointestinal-disorders/constipation)
* [腹泻](/professional/gastroenterology/symptoms-of-gastrointestinal-disorders/diarrhea)
* [胀气和腹胀](/professional/gastroenterology/symptoms-of-gastrointestinal-disorders/gas-related-symptoms)
* [腹痛](/professional/gastroenterology/acute-abdomen-and-surgical-gastroenterology/acute-abdominal-pain)
* [直肠疼痛或出血](/professional/gastroenterology/anorectal-disorders/proctitis)

与上消化道症状一样，下消化道症状也是由生理疾病引起，或代表功能障碍，例如 [肠易激综合症](/professional/gastroenterology/irritable-bowel-syndrome-ibs/irritable-bowel-syndrome-ibs) （即，即使经过广泛的评估也没有发现放射学、生化或病理学异常）。出现功能性症状的原因尚不明确。 研究证据表明，功能性症状患者可能有动力失调、痛觉障碍，或两者皆有；对于常人可耐受的感觉，如肠腔扩张、肠蠕动，上述患者可有明显不适。
